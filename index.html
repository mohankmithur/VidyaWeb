01 / About the Samprathishtaana

A living legacy of learning and service.

Founded on 7 November 1993 by the family, students and well-wishers of the late Vedic scholar Mitturu Purohitha Thimmayya Bhatta, the Samprathishtaana works to carry forward the ideals he lived by.

Its work brings together Vedic and Sanskrit education, thoughtful publishing, student assistance, public lectures, cultural programmes and support for people facing hardship.

01

Vedic and Sanskrit learning

Develop and share programmes in Sanskrit, Vedic and secular literature; encourage study through lectures, conferences and demonstrations.

Books, research and libraries

Publish educational books and periodicals; support research, preserve manuscripts and make a study library accessible to learners.

03

Students and opportunity

Support students through scholarships and learning resources, and help provide food and accommodation where needed.

04

A stronger, healthier society

Advance social, intellectual, physical and cultural wellbeing, and offer assistance in education, health and times of hardship.

Recognise and encourage excellence

Honour meaningful contributions in humanities, science and other fields, and nurture Indian knowledge and cultural traditions.

04 / Publications

A library of living knowledge.

The Samprathishtaana’s publications unfold across Vedic, Sanskrit, devotional, cultural and wellbeing themes, offering books for study, reflection and public learning.

01

Vedic and ritual studies

Scriptural texts, ritual instruction and foundational discussions drawn from the Vedic tradition.

02

Sanskrit and scholarly learning

Grammar, language study, textual analysis and research-oriented learning for students and scholars.

03

Puranic and devotional literature

Stories, ethical reflection and devotional writings linking heritage with everyday practice.

04

Culture, biography and memory

Biographical studies, cultural reflections and publications that preserve the memory of teachers and traditions.

The published works of Samprathishtaana are available for study, reference and circulation among readers who value traditional learning and cultural knowledge. A fuller catalogue, including subject categories and ordering information, is available on the publication page.
