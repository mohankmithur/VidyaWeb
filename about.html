01 / About the Samprathishtaana

A living legacy of learning and service.

Founded on 7 November 1993 by the family, students and well-wishers of the late Vedic scholar Mitturu Purohitha Thimmayya Bhatta, the Samprathishtaana works to carry forward the ideals he lived by.

Its work brings together Vedic and Sanskrit education, thoughtful publishing, student assistance, public lectures, cultural programmes and support for people facing hardship.

Mission and method

Education, service and continuity.

01

Teach and guide

Encourage study in Vedic knowledge, Sanskrit and living cultural practice through teaching, lectures and scholarship.

02

Publish and preserve

Share books, texts and learning resources in ways that make knowledge accessible to students, families and communities.

03

Serve with care

Support vulnerable families, learners and communities with practical help, health outreach and social concern.
